Legend of the Month

KING OF OLYMPUS

Zeus

Ruler of the Olympian gods, Zeus commanded the sky and thunder. His symbols include the thunderbolt, eagle, and royal scepter. He presided over divine order, hospitality, and justice, though his myths also tell of conflicts and complicated relationships.
